What this page says

A thermometer measures temperature. The zero line is where water freezes. When the red goes below the zero line, the temperature is a negative number.

Eight degrees below zero is written −8.

Design notes · for grown-ups

Soft, friendly, and still honest

This is a third direction, not an edit of the second. Nothing structural changed: the same eight sections, the same aspect-locked plane, the same rules about Spanish and about what Nova may say. What changed is the surface.

Emoji as vocabulary, not as garnish

Each of the eight sections has one emoji and one pastel tint, fixed everywhere they appear — in the step row, on the sticker shelf, in the page map, in the teacher's prep list. A learner who cannot yet read Important Words can still find 💬 purple. That is picture-plus-word pairing, the oldest move in sheltered instruction.

StepEmojiCodeEnglishSpanishPages, G4 L3

Three rules keep emoji from becoming noise. No control is ever labelled by an emoji alone — every one sits beside real text. Decorative emoji carry aria-hidden so a screen reader does not read "house emoji" before "Today". And because emoji render differently on Windows, Android and iOS, no emoji is ever the only carrier of a meaning — colour and text always say it too.

The teacher view deliberately has none

Decorative emoji stop at the student boundary. A teacher scanning 24 rows at 7:50am needs exact numbers and quiet surfaces, not a colour story. It is the same reason p(mastery) 0.61 reaches a student as three filled pips and the words "Getting there": one model, said twice, in the register each reader can act on.

Palette and type

Colour

Eight pastel tints, one per section, on a soft lavender ground with white cards and layered shadows instead of outlines. Semantic colour stays separate from the section tints: mint means mastered, sun means in progress, peach means needs attention.

Type

A rounded geometric display face over the project's own Nunito Sans body stack, set at 16.5px with a generous 1.65 line height. No licensed font is embedded; emoji come from the platform's own colour font.
