What this page says

A thermometer measures temperature. The zero line is where water freezes. When the red goes below the zero line, the temperature is a negative number.

Eight degrees below zero is written −8.

Try saying it:
The temperature is ____ degrees below zero, so I write ____. La temperatura es ____ grados bajo cero, entonces escribo ____.

Design notes · for grown-ups

What changed, and what did not

This direction makes the student side playful without loosening a single fact. Everything structural from the first direction is still here; what changed is who each surface is dressed for.

Two registers, one product

Kids — 3px ink outlines, hard offset shadows, 56px controls, 17px body type, saturated colour, short sentences, and no decimals anywhere. A nine-year-old should want to open it.

Grown-ups — same palette, hairline borders, no bounce, dense tables, exact numbers. A teacher scanning 24 students at 7:50am does not need a game board.

The split is not decoration. It is the same reason p(mastery) 0.61 shows as three filled pips to a student and as 0.61 to their teacher: one model, said two ways, in the register each reader can act on.

The trail is the section spine, wearing a costume

The eight stops are the same eight sections, in source order, with the same bilingual names — because all 29 lessons share that sequence. Drawing them as a journey along a number line is honest twice over: it is the lesson's real structure, and the number line is the object this curriculum teaches.

StopCodeEnglishSpanishPages, G4 L3

Stops are equal width with the page count written on them, never sized proportionally — Learn It carries 21 of Grade 5 Lesson 4's 54 pages while Play It carries exactly 1. Page-level position stays in the bar and in the on-demand overlay.

What playfulness is not allowed to touch

RuleStill true here
No invented SpanishCards with no source Spanish flip to a red-marked English face. The lesson title carries its fallback mark. Nova's Spanish is styled as Nova talking.
Nova scaffolds, never answers, on TI / TS / FQThe Practice screen's feedback nudges toward the left-of-zero idea and stops short of the answer.
The authored plane is 800 × 415Aspect-locked at 1.928:1 on every screen size. No cropping, no letterbox into 16:9.
One Next, one Back, progress reported onceThe trail shows stops; the bar shows pages. Neither repeats the other.
Rewards stay tied to real structureEight stickers, one per section. No coins, no gems, no streak-loss pressure — a struggling learner does not need a slot machine.
48px touch-target floorRaised to 56px on primary student controls. The project already chose 48 over WCAG's 24 for small hands.

The palette and the type

Colour

Ink moved from navy to a deep grape (#241c52) and the ground to a sunlit cream — warmer, still high contrast for young and EL readers. Nova keeps the indigo and cyan sampled from the source artwork. Semantic colour stays separate from decoration: grass means mastered, sun means progress, melon means needs attention and the negative side of a number line.

Type

A rounded geometric display face carries the kid voice, echoing the Bauhaus-family type inside the original lesson artwork without embedding a licensed font. Body type sits at 17px — up from 16 — because young readers and English learners both read better with room.
