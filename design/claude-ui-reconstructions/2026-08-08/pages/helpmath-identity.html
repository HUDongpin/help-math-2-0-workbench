A helping hand,
redrawn.

HELP Math 1.0 put two hands on a blue square — one gold, one outlined beside it. That outlined hand is the whole program in a single shape: someone is helping you. 2.0 keeps it and changes one thing. The helping hand is now made of light.

The mark

Blue field, HELP above, MATH below, two hands between them — the 1.0 arrangement, unchanged. Everything new happens inside it.

Lineage

What carried over, what moved

Kept

  • The blue square, and the rounded corner.
  • HELP above the art, MATH below it.
  • Orange for both words.
  • Two hands, and the size difference between them.
  • The learner’s hand in gold.
  • The helping hand as an outline, not a fill.

Changed

  • The helping hand is now an outline of light.
  • A plus sits in the gap where they meet.
  • The field gained a gradient.
  • The hands stand clear of each other, with the plus in the gap.
  • The wordmark was redrawn as outlines, so it never depends on a font being installed.
Rationale

Three decisions carry the whole argument

The learner is bigger

The helper stands to the side and smaller. AI supports; it does not lead. Two hands at equal size would say something we do not mean, and a district reading this logo for the next decade would be right to hold us to it.

A plus, not a sparkle

The four-point sparkle is this year’s stock symbol for AI and will date like a modem icon. A plus is the same brightness, means positive on its face, and is the first operator these students ever learned. It is the only pure-white thing in the mark, and it touches neither hand.

The orange doesn’t move

HELP Math has been in classrooms for years. Teachers coming back should recognise it before they read it, so the heritage orange stays at full strength while everything around it modernises.

Behaviour

It has to survive being small

Three cuts of the same mark, each dropping the detail that turns to mud at the next size down: the fingertip nodes go, then the helper hand itself. Nothing is redrawn — only removed.

Alternates

Two other directions, built out

Both are single-hand marks — smaller stories, but stronger at icon sizes. Worth keeping as sub-brand marks even if the two-hand lockup stays primary.

B — In Your Hand

The plus moves into the palm. Nothing behind, nothing to lose at 16 px — the cleanest app icon of the three.

C — Counting Constellation

Five fingertips as nodes on a rising line. The most literal about maths; the weakest below 48 px.

Handling

Rules and files

Clear space
The cap height of HELP on every side — 92 units in the 512 grid, or 20% of the mark’s width.
Minimum sizes
Primary lockup 64 px. Mark 32 px. Below 24 px, use the favicon; the two-hand composition turns to mud there.
Don’t re-letter it
The wordmark is outlines, not type. Setting it live in Fredoka will not match, and will break wherever the font isn’t installed.
Don’t equalise the hands
Same size, or the helper in front, inverts the meaning.
Don’t place it on photography
Give it flat blue, ink, or paper.
Don’t move the plus onto a hand
It belongs in the gap. That gap is what makes the mark read as helper + learner.
