# G4 L11 source-domain specifications

This namespace holds source-bound inspection specifications for independently
identified Flash frame domains. It is deliberately separate from
`src/timelines`, renderers, the animation registry, and whole-lesson routing.

`course-g04-l11-in-010.ts` describes and inspects the source-proven
`sprite-246` local domain only. Its inspection result is structural evidence,
not JavaScript playback, original-runtime evidence, audio acceptance, or a
candidate/strict/publication claim.

`course-g04-l11-ti-003-predecessor.ts` is deliberately earlier in the
evidence chain: it records the source-static predecessor facts for
`sprite-346`, including its fractional native stage and record-only AVM1
markers, while keeping the domain itself undeclared. It cannot supply a root
playhead, capture identity, natural trace, JavaScript candidate, or acceptance
result. `sprite-345` remains an explicitly unresolved child obligation rather
than a substituted or inferred domain.

`course-g04-l11-ti-003-sprite345-predecessor.ts` separately records the
accepted read-only proposal for the `sprite-345` child: 55 local frames, four
source labels, six record-only AVM1 action bodies, its nested placement chain,
and the structural embedded MP3 stream. It preserves the fractional native
stage and the 800×600 capture raster as distinct facts. The planner’s one
accepted structural source proof has not materialized a successor: `sprite-345`
and its parent `sprite-346` remain undeclared, while entry timing, random
selection behavior, cue semantics, bilingual content, Replay, scoring,
runtime closure, authoring inspection, JavaScript, review, strict completion,
and publication all remain blocked.

`course-g04-l11-gs-003-predecessor.ts` records the SWF-only GS003
`sprite-231` predecessor facts: its direct named root placement, labels,
record-only AVM1 body hashes, exact source-proven 33/1/32/27/6 partition, and
four embedded-stream obligations. It keeps the successor proof absent and the
domain undeclared. In particular, structural inspection supplies no game,
randomness, score, Replay, audio, runtime, candidate, fidelity, acceptance, or
publication authority.

`course-g04-l11-fq-003-predecessor.ts` preserves the historical FQ003
`sprite-910` predecessor from the accepted read-only proposal: its 72 local
frames, 54 labels, three record-only AVM1 records, root placement, source
bindings, and explicit quiz, score, Replay, host, child-domain, and
bilingual-audio blockers. The later declaration receipt consumed that exact
predecessor; this module remains a historical source-static inspection and is
not rewritten into current state.

`course-g04-l11-fq-003.ts` binds the materialized `sprite-910` source proof,
the exact successor manifest/inventory/disposition, and applied receipt. It
exposes deterministic structural inspection for all 72 local frames in EN and
ES while returning no root playhead, runtime entry, capture identity, executed
AVM1 result, renderer, registry entry, or Current-JavaScript state. The other
212 child timelines remain unresolved, and all runtime, quiz, score, Replay,
audio, fidelity, review, strict-completion, release, and publication authority
remains closed.

`course-g04-l11-in-010-residual-predecessor.ts` records the accepted
read-only planner's nine source-proven local-action candidates below the
already-persisted `sprite-246` domain, alongside five retained unresolved
timelines. It preserves the current 2-declared/14-unresolved state and only a
conditional 11/5 forecast; it neither materializes a successor nor authorizes
runtime, audio, JavaScript, registry, fidelity, acceptance, strict-completion,
or publication behavior.

`course-g04-l11-fq-001-predecessor.ts` is a machine-only display-list and
source-static predecessor for release member 41/44. It binds the preserved
FLA/SWF, the current source-static module, coverage-v2 file, two root trace
specifications, verified-subset trace index, and structural keyframes. Its
query returns only one-indexed root-frame records. The bound physical census
contains 68 definitions and 142 `PlaceObject2` tags: two in root, eight in
`sprite-22`, and 132 in `sprite-68`. The reachable `sprite-22` and `sprite-68`
timelines remain unresolved, five FUI timelines retain unresolved reachability,
and every runtime, audio, implementation, fidelity, review, completion,
release, and publication gate remains closed.
